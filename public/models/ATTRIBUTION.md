# Juno spacecraft visualization

- Local file: `nasa-juno.glb` (9,185,648 bytes, glTF 2.0; textures and buffers embedded).
- Title: **Juno 3D Model**.
- Credit: **NASA Visualization Technology Applications and Development (VTAD)**, as listed on the official resource page.
- Official source: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/juno-3d-model/
- Download: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/psd/solar/2023/09/j/Juno.glb?emrc=6af93541401ce
- NASA 3D Resources describes its collection as free and without copyright: https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources
- Usage guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

Downloaded 15 November 2026. The binary is retained unmodified. Runtime display normalizes its size and centers it; it is used as a **visual replica for a fictional educational instrument mission**, not a claim that the player launches the real Juno or that Juno is present at a particular current location. No NASA endorsement is implied. Do not imply endorsement or reuse NASA insignia as the game's own identity.

The model loads locally in MEDIUM/HIGH graphics during the Jupiter probe operation. LOW graphics and loading/failure states use original procedural geometry. No remote decoder is required: the included GLB has no Draco dependency and no external image/buffer URI.
